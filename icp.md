# Context for Claude (edit this, it drives filtering and scoring)

## Topic
Posts about the secondary market for private company shares and funds: secondaries, GP-led and LP-led
transactions, continuation funds, employee liquidity, tender offers, secondary pricing and volumes.
Not relevant: job ads, generic "secondary market" mentions about consumer goods, crypto token trading
unless it is about private-company equity, pure spam or engagement bait.

## ICP (who we want to reach)
- Decision makers and influencers at: VC and growth funds, family offices, wealth managers, fund of funds,
  secondary buyers and intermediaries, late-stage startups with employee liquidity needs (CFO, Head of
  People/Finance, founders).
- Seniority: Partner, Principal, Director, VP, Head of, C-level, Founder.
- Low fit: students, recruiters, vendors selling to our ICP, competitors, people with no business role.

## Outreach tone
Short, specific, no flattery, no emojis, no em dashes. Reference the actual post. Never invent facts.
